BloomSize
Under 10 bits per item buys a 1% wrong-yes rate. Then it runs a real filter to prove it.
Open the app
1 million items at a 1% false positive rate need 9.59 bits per item, 7 hash functions and about 1.14 MiB. Put in twice as many items and the rate jumps past 10%.

What it does

  • Bits, bytes and hash count for your item count and target rate
  • What a fixed memory budget gets you, and how many items it holds
  • What happens when the filter is overfilled
  • A real bit-array simulation next to the formula
Formulas from the Wikipedia Bloom filter page; exact small-filter values are counted, not approximated. Static, client-side, nothing stored. Source
